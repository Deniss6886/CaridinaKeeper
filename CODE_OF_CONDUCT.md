# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include being respectful, welcoming different viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, personal attacks, public or private intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer listed in the repository security/contact channels. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This text is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
